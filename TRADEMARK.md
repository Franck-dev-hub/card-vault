# Trademark policy

The code in this repository is released under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE).\
The AGPL covers the code, not the Card Vault name and logo: they stay with the
Card Vault authors listed in the [README](README.md#authors).

## What you can do

- Fork, modify and run the code, as the AGPL allows.
- Name Card Vault in plain text to say where your project comes from, for
  example "based on Card Vault".

## What needs written permission

- Using "Card Vault" or its logo in the name, domain or branding of a product or
  service.
- Suggesting that a project or an instance is official, endorsed by Card Vault,
  or affiliated with it.

A deployed fork must use its own name and logo.

## Third-party marks

Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering and the other games' names and card images belong
to their owners.\
Card Vault is not affiliated with them or endorsed by them.
